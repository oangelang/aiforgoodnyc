Why we exist
AI

AI has the potential to solve
the world's hardest problems —
if we build it right.

The tools exist. The data exists. What's missing is the bridge — between the people building AI and the people who need it most. Between technical capability and lived experience. Between speed-to-market and responsibility to communities.

We exist to close that gap. Not by slowing AI down, but by making sure the right people are in the room — asking the right questions, building with the right values, and directing resources toward the problems that actually matter.

Disaster Response
4 hrs
Lahaina fire damage mapped same day

After the 2023 Maui wildfires, Microsoft AI for Good Lab assessed structural damage across the entire impact zone in four hours at 97% accuracy. The Red Cross had actionable maps the same day.

Global Health
350K
Deaths a year that AI could help prevent

Anthropic and the Gates Foundation are using AI to accelerate HPV therapy screening — 90% of those 350,000 annual deaths occur in low- and middle-income countries where diagnostics are most limited.
